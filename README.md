# Neopets Enhance

Neopets, your way, in your own theme. A userscript for the beta site:

- **Menus you can change**: every menu in the top bar, folders, your own links, a Profile menu, and Switch Pet.
- **Dailies board**: every daily at a glance, what's ready, a run bar to do them in a row, and reminders.
- **Neoboards and Neomail made over**: cards in your theme, what's new since you last read, board colours of your own, a reply box with every smilie and a preview, and Neomail in a little window.
- **Items**: buttons under items to look for them (Shop Wizard, Trading Post, Auctions, SDB, JellyNeo, itemdb…), with prices and NC values.
- **And more**: Go to any page by its name (Ctrl+K), a Shop Dock, a better QuickRef, a random event log, a fitting tab icon for every page, clocks, and old pages in the beta look.

Everything has a switch in its Settings (the gear in the top bar).

## Install

1. Install [Tampermonkey](https://www.tampermonkey.net/) in your browser.
2. Open **[neopets-enhance.user.js](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Legacy-l/neopets-enhance/main/neopets-enhance.user.js)** and click **Install**.
3. Go to Neopets. The script updates itself from here.

Already have it installed? Open it in Tampermonkey's editor, paste the new code over the old and save. Your settings stay.
